## Other bundled runtime components

EmulatorJS includes NippleJS (MIT) for touch controls and Socket.IO client (MIT) for optional emulator features. Their license texts are at `emulator/licenses/nipplejs-MIT.txt` and `emulator/licenses/socketio-MIT.txt`. Their upstream projects are [NippleJS](https://github.com/yoannmoinet/nipplejs) and [Socket.IO](https://github.com/socketio/socket.io).

The emulator cores, ROM, and site code are separate components with separate licenses. Arcade Atlas does not include commercial game ROMs or console BIOS files.
